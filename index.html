DimerForge

Build stacked dimers from a single .xyz structure, or take molecules and real neighbouring pairs from a crystal (.cif). Set the interplanar distance, slip, tilt and twist in the molecule's own frame, edit the molecule in the designer, check the contacts, and export the monomer or the dimer as .xyz, PDB, MOL/SDF, Gaussian or ORCA input. Drag to rotate the view, scroll to zoom, or drop a file onto the viewer.

Arrow keys rotate the view (hold Shift for small steps), plus and minus zoom, and 0 resets the view. The readouts below the viewer describe the structure. In the Design tab, select atoms by typing their numbers; in the Crystal tab, use the lists.

Placement of copy B

B is rotated about A's centroid (twist, then tilt about long, then tilt about short), then shifted by slip and d. d is the height of B's centroid above A's plane.

Molecular designer

Click atoms in the viewer to select them, or type their numbers. Shift-click adds an atom to the selection, or removes it. Edits change molecule A, and the dimer follows.

Element

Groups

Select one atom. A selected H or halogen is replaced by the group. On any other atom, the group goes in a free position.

Geometry

Start from a template

Tidy relaxes the atoms you added or re-elemented (and any you select) towards typical bond lengths and angles, and pushes apart clashing atoms. Other atoms keep their geometry. Shortcuts: Delete, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, Esc.

Crystal structure

Open or paste a CIF file to pick a molecule, or a real neighbouring pair, from the crystal. The file needs the cell, the symmetry operations and the fractional coordinates.

Molecules in the cell

Neighbours of the selected molecule

Every neighbour with a contact within the vdW sum + 0.5 Å. Pairs related by symmetry are merged. Click a molecule in the viewer to select it, or click a pair to show it.
